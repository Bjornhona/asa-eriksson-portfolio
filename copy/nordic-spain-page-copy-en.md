## THE PROBLEM

### Three things Nordic companies get wrong in Spain

**They assume EU-wide compliance is enough.**
GDPR is European. LSSI-CE, LOPDGDD and the AEPD's cookie criteria are Spanish, and they are stricter and more specifically enforced than most Nordic companies expect. A cookie banner that passes in Stockholm frequently fails in Madrid.

**They don't know accessibility became mandatory.**
Ley 11/2023 transposed the European Accessibility Act into Spanish law, effective 28 June 2025. Obligations now extend to private companies — e-commerce, banking, booking platforms, passenger transport. The standard is WCAG 2.2 AA. Sanctions run from €301 to €1,000,000. Very few private Spanish websites currently meet it.

**They translate instead of localise.**
Spanish B2B buyers read a translated site as a foreign company testing the market. Missing NIF and physical address, no Bizum, formal register wrong for the audience, a contact form nobody answers in August — each one costs conversions in ways analytics won't show you.

## FAQ

**We already have a website. Do we need a new one?**
Usually not. Most problems are fixable on your existing site, whatever it's built with. That's what the remediation tier is for — I'd rather fix what you have than sell you a rebuild you don't need.

**Does this apply to us? We're small.**
The accessibility obligations under Ley 11/2023 generally exempt companies under 10 employees. RGPD, LOPDGDD and the Spanish cookie rules do not exempt anyone. Small companies still need the legal layer.

**Do you work outside Barcelona?**
Yes — anywhere in Spain, and the work is largely remote regardless.

**We're not Nordic. Can you still help?**
Yes. The compliance work is the same for any foreign company entering Spain. The Nordic part is just where I'm most useful on the cultural side.
